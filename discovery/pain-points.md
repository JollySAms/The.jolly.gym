# Pain Points Research: Trainer/Trainee Management Apps

## Trainer Pain Points

### 1. Tool Sprawl
Trainers pay for 3–5 disconnected tools that don't talk to each other — one for workouts, another for bookings, a third for email, a fourth for payments.
- Trainers spend up to **5 hours/week** on manual data work
- Quote: "By the time they realise they need a proper system, they are already paying for three or four tools that barely talk to each other."

### 2. Per-Client Pricing Punishes Growth
Every major platform charges per active client. The better a trainer does, the more their software costs.
- Trainerize: ~$60-70/mo at 30 clients
- TrueCoach: $106.99/mo capped at 50 clients
- PT Distinction: $89.90/mo for 50 clients then $1.60/client

### 3. Overly Complex UX
- "Interface can feel dated and cluttered" — Trainerize (Capterra)
- "Steep learning curve with overwhelming feature set"
- "Older adults and non-tech-savvy clients struggle with the interface"
- "Too many platforms create more admin than they remove." — PTDC

### 4. Client App Experience is Poor
- Clients resist or abandon the trainee-facing app
- Apps that "look like they were designed in 2010"
- "Clients are confused more than helped" — Trainerize users
- Push notifications are the *only* reason many clients check their schedule

### 5. Scheduling is Manually Painful
- No clean "assign workout to session slot + let client confirm" flow
- Workout programming and calendar scheduling are separate tools
- Quote: "Having clients text at all times trying to get on the schedule is problematic."

### 6. Bugs and Reliability (Trainerize post-ABC acquisition)
- "Load lag, videos load slowly if at all… chat breaks"
- "Lacks an undo button and refreshing without saving causes loss of all edits"
- Trustpilot 3.6/5: "Constantly glitching and dated", billing issues, no visible cancellation

### 7. Missing Attendance Features
- No session RSVP / attendance confirmation from clients
- No cancellation/no-show workflow built in
- No-shows are among the most frustrating parts of the job — no software handles this

## Trainee Pain Points

### 1. Don't Know What They're Supposed to Do
- Forget what workout is scheduled for which day
- Programs delivered as PDFs or inside clunky apps they rarely open
- No clear "what am I doing today" view

### 2. Forget Sessions / No Easy Cancellation
- No easy mechanism to confirm, reschedule, or cancel in advance
- Text trainers on WhatsApp because in-app messaging is clunky

### 3. App Complexity
- Non-tech-savvy clients find apps overwhelming
- Need simple view: here is your session, here is your workout, tap to confirm

## Key Themes
1. **Fragmented toolchain** — mentioned across every source
2. **Poor client/trainee app** — universally underserved
3. **No integrated scheduling + attendance** — biggest functional gap
4. **Per-client pricing** — top complaint from growing trainers
5. **Bloated, dated UX** — incumbents built for features, not usability

## Opportunity Score: HIGH
The core loop (trainer plans workout → assigns to session → trainee RSVPs) is a genuine white space. Every competitor either skips scheduling or makes it a premium add-on.
