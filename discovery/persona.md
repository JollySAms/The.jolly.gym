# User Personas

## Primary Persona: The Trainer

**Name:** Jolmer
**Role:** Independent personal trainer, 4 groups of 4–9 clients per group
**Problem:** No single app to plan workouts in a calendar, add exercises to those workouts, and track progression (sets, reps, weight) per client across group sessions.

### Current State
- Uses: Trainerize + SuperSaas + WhatsApp
- Frustrated by: Having to message clients individually to check attendance, no clear overview of sessions and groups in one place, switching between apps constantly
- Spends: Significant time on admin that should be automated

### Desired State
- Wants: One place to plan sessions, assign workouts to groups, see who's coming and who's not, track exercise progression per client (weight, reps, sets), and easily create/edit workouts with all necessary detail
- Values: Simplicity, full overview, no app-switching
- Would pay: Flat monthly rate

### Quote
> "I just want one app where I can plan a session, add the exercises with sets and reps, see who's showing up, and track how each client is progressing. That's it."

## Secondary Persona: The Trainee

**Name:** Poelie
**Role:** Gym-goer, 1–2 sessions/week with a PT
**Problem:** No easy way to confirm attendance, track workouts, see progression, or view upcoming sessions in one app.

### Current State
- Uses: Trainer's chosen app (reluctantly) + WhatsApp
- Frustrated by: Clunky apps, not knowing what's coming, guilt around no-shows
- Spends: 0 time proactively — only reacts when the trainer reaches out

### Desired State
- Wants: Simple notification, quick confirm/cancel, see what workout is planned, view personal progression
- Values: Ease of use, not having to learn yet another app
- Would pay: Nothing — this is the trainer's tool; trainee access must be frictionless

### Quote
> "I just want a notification that says 'you have a session Thursday at 7am — are you coming?' and I tap yes or no. That's it."
