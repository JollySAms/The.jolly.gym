# Competitor Analysis: Trainer/Trainee Management Apps

## 1. Trainerize (ABC Trainerize)
- **URL:** trainerize.com
- **Tagline:** "Train clients online and in-person"
- **Pricing:** Free (1 client) → $23/mo (5 clients) → $100+/mo (200 clients) + add-ons
- **Key Features:** Workout builder, MyFitnessPal integration, in-app messaging, nutrition tracking
- **Missing:**
  - No scheduling/appointment calendar in base plans
  - No client attendance/RSVP flow
  - No undo button; crashes frequently
  - Dated, cluttered UI
- **Target:** Independent trainers to mid-size gyms
- **Rating:** Trustpilot 3.6/5

## 2. TrueCoach
- **URL:** truecoach.co
- **Pricing:** $19.99/mo (5 clients) → $52.99/mo (20) → $106.99/mo (50) — no unlimited tier
- **Key Features:** Workout builder, 850+ video library, progress tracking, messaging, wearables
- **Missing:**
  - iOS only — no Android support
  - No scheduling / session booking
  - No nutrition (relies on MFP)
  - Feels like "a digital PDF, not a modern platform"
- **Target:** 1:1 personal trainers, online coaches

## 3. PT Distinction
- **URL:** ptdistinction.com
- **Pricing:** $19.90/mo (3 clients) → $59.90/mo (25) → $89.90/mo (50) + $1.60/extra client
- **Key Features:** AI workout builder, nutrition + meal planning, assessments, group training, automation, habit tracking, custom-branded apps
- **Missing:**
  - Expensive at scale
  - Steeper learning curve
  - Overwhelming for smaller trainers
- **Target:** Online personal trainers

## 4. Wodify
- **URL:** wodify.com
- **Pricing:** $79/mo (Essentials) — upper tiers require demo call
- **Key Features:** Class scheduling, billing, client management, waivers, CRM, leaderboards
- **Missing:**
  - Not designed for 1:1 trainer-client relationships
  - Workout programming only in Ultimate tier
  - Overkill for independent trainers
- **Target:** CrossFit boxes, boutique fitness studios, NOT individual trainers

## 5. Everfit
- **URL:** everfit.io
- **Pricing:** Free (5 clients) → Pro from $16/mo → Studio $88/mo
- **Key Features:** AI workout builder, nutrition + habit coaching, macro tracking, meal plans, messaging, payments, white-label
- **Missing:**
  - Modular pricing — real cost rises fast
  - Complex; can create more admin than it removes
  - Mixed support and billing reviews
- **Target:** Independent personal trainers and online coaches

## Market Gaps

1. **Integrated scheduling + workout assignment** — nobody connects these cleanly
2. **Trainee RSVP / attendance flow** — completely missing from all competitors
3. **Clean trainee-first app** — every platform is built for trainers, not clients
4. **Flat pricing** — per-client model is universally hated
5. **Android parity** — TrueCoach iOS-only; others have poor Android apps
6. **Simple, focused UX** — market split between over-complex and too basic
